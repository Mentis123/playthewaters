# 03 — The name

*24 Oct 2026. Working title: Reel Mobile. Owner's candidate: "The Waters". Research was web search plus DNS resolution from the sandbox: **no USPTO/EUIPO query, no WHOIS, no social-handle check.** Run a real trademark search and a registrar check before anything is printed on a card.*

## Verdict

**"The Waters" is usable.** The only exact collisions are two tiny itch.io jam projects (a 3-hour Ren'Py visual novel, a PuzzleScript puzzle). No Steam, App Store or Play title found. Its weakness is not a collision, it is that "the waters" is a common phrase: it will not rank on the name alone, and it is a weak mark. Both are fixed the same way:

- **Title:** *The Waters*. Wordmark with the tag line **"six waters, one shore."**
- **Handles and domain:** a coined form so search and socials have a handle: `playthewaters` / `thewaters.app` (`.app` and `.game` showed no DNS; `.com` and `.io` resolve, so assume taken). Do **not** use "Reel" as the modifier: it ties the name back to the placeholder and the crowded *Reel Fishing* franchise (Natsume, since 1996).
- **In-game:** the legend fish are never called "loach" and the Ocarina pond is never named. *Loach* is Nintendo-adjacent as a nod and is also the real fish family and a film director's surname.

## What happens on a yes

1. Owner runs or commissions the trademark search (class 9 and 41) and registers `thewaters.app` (or the coined handle) before the alpha site changes its title.
2. `23_ALPHA_CANON` §9 is updated to the decided name; `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, the PWA manifest, the share card and `/alpha` on the personal site follow in one change.
3. The repo stays `reelmobile` until the alpha ships; renaming a repo mid-build buys nothing.
