# Pond reference photos

Real water to draw the rebuild's reference art from. Taken by the owner; EXIF stripped on import. Web copies and notes are also on the alpha site: https://adamrappaport.vercel.app/alpha

| File | What to take from it |
| ---- | -------------------- |
| `01-garden-pond.jpg` | The distance mechanic in one frame: fish read clearly near the rocks, the water turns opaque green a few metres out. Rounded stone bank, pines and maples on the far shore. |
| `02-castle-moat.jpg` | Still water as a mirror: sky and watchtower on the surface, not under it. Floating weed near the viewer shows depth. Hard stone far shore. |
| `03-clear-stone-pool.jpg` | The clearest water in the set: flagstone floor, fish shadows on the bottom, caustic light. The close-water look to aim for. |
| `04-lily-pond.jpg` | Brown, opaque water with lily pad islands and reed beds that break up the surface. Forested hillside far shore. |
| `05-fog-lake-pov.jpg` | The player's view: phone portrait, feet on the bank, one long body of water running to a far shore. Fog flattens the colour; ripples catch light down the middle. |
| `06-cattail-marsh.jpg` | Wind on the water: wide ripple pattern over blue, a calm golden band near the far reeds. Cattails in front, a cormorant drying its wings on a snag, autumn trees. |
