## 3. Binary rubric (B only, on the phone, after the gate). Pass: 18 of 22, every ★ item required.

### The bank
1. ★ The near edge of the water is a row of rounded stones, as in photo 01, not a flat strip.
2. Stones read as stone: lichen spots, mossy tops, darker where they meet the water.
3. The stones do not read as a repeating pattern of the same rock.
4. Low weeds and grass move a little in the wind; nothing sways in lockstep.
5. The ground between and behind the stones reads as grass and clover, not a flat colour band.
6. ★ The stones never hide a fish, the lure or the line in the water you can actually cast to.
7. The rod still reads as yours, in the bottom-right corner, in front of the bank.

### The shore and the far trees
8. Water darkens where it meets the stones (a wet edge), so the stones sit IN the water.
9. Reeds at the side banks are blades that sway, not cones.
10. ★ The far shore reads as layered trees (a hazier back row and a darker front row), not a flat painted strip.
11. The far trees agree with the light at all four lights.

### The air
12. Dawn has mist sitting on the far water; day has none.
13. Mist drifts slowly and never pops in or out.
14. Dusk and night have small midges or motes over the water; day and dawn have few or none.
15. Midges never read as UI, sparks or fish cues.

### Play and phone
16. ★ Cast, bite, fight and reveal feel exactly as A1.
17. ★ A fish cue (ripple, wake, roll) is as readable as in A1 at every light.
18. ★ Draw calls < 100 and triangles < 80,000, read back from the renderer on the phone profile (the A2 e2e check).
19. No visible frame drops over 10 minutes on the owner's iPhone.
20. The phone is not noticeably warmer after 10 minutes than on A1.
21. The leave glyph (top right, while reading the water) returns to the title, and starting again works.
22. Nothing new flickers at rest.
